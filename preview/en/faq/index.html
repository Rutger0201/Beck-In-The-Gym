FAQ

Good to know.

Can I start without experience?+

Yes. We start at your level and take time to explain exercises and equipment. No experience or particular fitness level is required.

Where do sessions take place?+

Mainly at DeMIX Fitness & Leefstijl in Almere. Other locations may be possible by arrangement. Ask about the location's access requirements when we meet.

Can I train with someone else?+

Yes, through duo coaching or a small group of up to four people. Guidance takes differences in experience into account.

What happens during the intake?+

We get to know each other, discuss your goals and consider your experience and current level. A body-composition measurement can be discussed. We then make a suitable plan.

Do you provide nutrition and recovery advice?+

We discuss general information that supports your training. BITG does not provide medical treatment or specialist nutritional treatment. For those needs, an appropriate specialist is the right choice.

How often should I train?+

That depends on your goals, experience and schedule. We find a manageable frequency and build it up where appropriate.

Which ages do you coach?+

People of all ages are welcome to discuss the possibilities. Coaching is adapted to the individual; a parent or guardian is involved for minors.

How do I request coaching?+

Use the contact form or a direct contact channel. We start with an introductory conversation. There are no bookings or payments through this website.
