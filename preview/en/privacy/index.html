PRIVACY

Your details, handled with care.

Who is responsible

Beck In The Gym is responsible for contact requests through this website. Final privacy contact details will be added before publication.

What we use and why

A request contains your name, email address and training goal. Phone number, training experience, coaching preference and message are optional. We use these details to answer your question and discuss possible coaching at your request. We do not automatically subscribe you to marketing.

No medical details in this form

Please do not submit diagnoses, medical details or other sensitive information. Intake and any body-composition measurement are discussed separately, not collected through the public contact form.

Who can access your information

Only authorised administrators can view requests. Hosting and email providers process data to operate the website. The proposed hosting is Render in Frankfurt; Brevo handles transactional email. Final processors and agreements are reviewed before launch. WhatsApp and Instagram only receive data when you choose to open their links.

Retention

Contact requests are removed from request management no later than six months after the last substantive contact. Necessary client details, if you become a client, have a separate purpose and retention policy. Financial administration has its own legal retention requirements. Backups may retain deleted data for up to thirty days.

Cookies and statistics

This website does not use advertising tracking and uses a dark appearance only. No appearance preference is stored. Limited visitor statistics are enabled only after the privacy settings have been reviewed. We measure pages and contact actions, never form contents. Tracking requiring consent remains disabled until that consent is obtained.

Security and your rights

Access controls, encrypted connections and backups protect your information. You may request access, correction or deletion, and object or request restriction where applicable. Use the privacy contact details to exercise your rights. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority.
