Frequently asked questions↗
Can I start without experience?+

Yes. We start at your level and take time to explain exercises and equipment. No experience or particular fitness level is required.

Where do sessions take place?+

Mainly at DeMIX Fitness & Leefstijl in Almere. Other locations may be possible by arrangement. Ask about the location's access requirements when we meet.

Can I train with someone else?+

Yes, through duo coaching or a small group of up to four people. Guidance takes differences in experience into account.

What happens during the intake?+

We get to know each other, discuss your goals and consider your experience and current level. A body-composition measurement can be discussed. We then make a suitable plan.
